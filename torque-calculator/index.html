Torque & Rotational Motion Calculator

AP Physics C • Uses τ = rF sinθ and (optional) α = τ / I

This AP Physics C practice tool computes rotational torque using τ = rF sin(θ). Enter force magnitude, lever arm length, and the angle between r and F to get torque magnitude and direction.

Use the direction toggle (CCW + / CW −) to match your sign convention. Optionally enter moment of inertia I to compute angular acceleration α = τ / I. Runs entirely in your browser—no data is saved.

How to Use

  • Enter force magnitude (N) and lever arm length (m).
  • Enter θ as the angle between the lever arm vector r (pivot → point of application) and the force vector F.
  • Select the torque direction based on your diagram: CCW (+) or CW (−).
  • Optional: enter moment of inertia I (kg·m²) to compute angular acceleration α.
  • Click Calculate to see results with units.

FAQ

Is this allowed on the AP exam?
No—this is for practice and checking work outside the exam.

Why does the angle use sin(θ)?
Only the component of F perpendicular to r creates torque.

Does this store my inputs?
No. Everything runs locally in your browser.

Tip: θ is the angle between the lever arm vector r (pivot → point of application) and the force F.
